03 / READ THE RELATIONSHIP

What does your model tell you?

These are training results, measured on the same rows used to fit the model. They do not measure accuracy on new data or establish cause and effect.

The relationship

How close are the predictions?

Points on the dashed line are predicted exactly. Points above it were underpredicted; points below it were overpredicted.

What does the model miss?

Residual = actual − predicted. Look for a curve or widening spread instead of a random cloud around zero.

How big are the errors?

Bars count rows by prediction error. Values near zero mean smaller errors; long tails reveal unusually large misses. This alone does not test all model assumptions.

Explain the coefficients

Each slope is the predicted change in the outcome for a one-unit increase in that predictor, holding other included predictors constant. The intercept is the predicted outcome when all predictors are zero, which may be outside the data’s range.

Coefficients use their original units; their sizes do not rank predictor importance. This browser analyzer reports descriptive OLS estimates, without p-values or confidence intervals.
