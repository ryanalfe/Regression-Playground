Statistics is more fun when
you control the data.

I’m Ryan Alfe, a Statistics and Economics student at the University of Florida. I’m passionate about statistics, and I created this interactive learning environment to share that curiosity.

I want visitors to gain a deeper understanding of statistical regression by interacting with the data and model. Here, you can do more than read a formula, you can change the data, learn how it affects the model, and build an intuition for both the power and the limitations of a statistical regression model.

3

WHY I’M BUILDING THIS

A place to practice explaining the “why.”

For me, learning statistics includes learning to communicate it clearly. This project connects mathematical ideas with visual explanations, thoughtful questions, and honest limitations. My aim is to make the subject approachable while preserving the reasoning behind it.

This playground focuses on ordinary least-squares linear regression. The examples are synthetic, so you can experiment freely and focus on the ideas.
